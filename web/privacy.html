DevShire

Privacy

DevShire has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Sessions are deleted after 24 hours, or up to 7 days if the host resets the timer.

What is stored

  • The session's text or code, its Replay recording, and questions and answers.
  • Names viewers type in. A name is optional.
  • Your network address, for a short time, to stop spam and abuse. It is kept in memory only, never with the session.

Sessions are saved to a database so they survive server restarts.

Who can see a session

  • Anyone with the viewer link can watch the session and replay it from the start. Share it only with people you want watching.
  • Only the holder of the host link can write, hand over the pen or end the session. Keep it to yourself.
  • A question is seen only by the host and the person who asked it, until the host answers it.

When it is deleted

  • Right away, when the host clicks End session.
  • 6 hours after the host leaves.
  • 24 hours after the session started, or after the host last pressed Reset.
  • 7 days after the session started, whatever happens.

Deleted sessions cannot be recovered.

In your browser

DevShire uses no cookies. Your browser remembers your theme, your viewer name and your place in a session, and you can clear these at any time.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Links are random and cannot be guessed in practice. Everything viewers send is cleaned before it is shown.

Hosting

DevShire runs on Render, behind Cloudflare, with a Neon database. Like any website host, they process connection data to deliver pages.

How it works
  • Line and word changes: removed lines are red, added lines green, and the exact words that changed are marked.
  • Unchanged parts fold away. Click "unchanged lines" to show them. Jump between changes with ↑ ↓ or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓.
  • Merge: the → and ← arrows on a change copy it from one side to the other.
  • Ignore text: skip parts that always differ, like dates or IDs. Plain text or a regular expression.
  • Private: the comparison runs on your device. Your text is never sent to a server. Share links keep the text inside the link itself, so we never store it.
  • Discuss live: opens a DevShire session with the diff, so you can walk a teammate, student or candidate through the changes as you type.

Paste two versions of text or code. Changes show up as you type. Everything stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded or saved.
